## 5. Per area: they vs us

- Boot. [V] `xnuloader/loader.c:1-12`: own UEFI app (EFI without EDK2) that plays iBoot, finds an
  "XNUP" payload at `0xF0000000`. Booted by `fastboot boot` of their Mu-Silicium port + BootShim
  (`devices/poco-x5-pro/README.md:10-11`). Nothing is flashed. We use Mu UEFI + DarwinBoot.
  [I] No reason to change.
- GPU. [V] OpenGL ES through Mesa freedreno (`gpu=1`); "Metal: there is a shader compiler, no Metal"
  (`README.md:44`, `docs/air.md:3-8`). [V] `NativeAdrenoMetalPlugin.mm` is in our slop fork
  (`external/slop/NOTES.md:643`), not in HakiOS. [I] It is slop's own work, not from HakiOS.
- Mesa patches (we take 4, `scripts/gpu/env.sh:13`):
  - `freedreno-darwin`: present, `tools/mesa/freedreno-darwin.patch`. Changed only by the rename
    (`redwood-drm` -> `hakios-drm`, `hakios_drm.c` -> `hakios.drm.c`). Our `scripts/gpu/mesa-fetch.sh:57`
    expects `redwood-drm`, so the new file does not apply as is.

- Audio. [V] `userland/src/audio_plugin.c`: the diff to `b4c5513` is the rename only

## 7. Submodule pin: recommendation

Keep `external/hackios-xnu-userland` at its pin and mirror it (a fork under our account), because
the two Mesa patches `ir3-shift-split` and `ir3-ssbo-subdword` exist only there, the new
`freedreno-darwin.patch` uses the `hakios-drm` names that `scripts/gpu/mesa-fetch.sh` does not
expect, and `audio_plugin.c`/`eagl.c` did not change except for names. All files we take stay MIT.
The upstream repo now holds only a README, and GitHub can drop unreferenced commits after a history
rewrite, so a mirror protects `b4c5513`. Re-point the reference entry `hackios-xnu-kernel`
(`scripts/setup/get-references.sh:22`) to the new HEAD (`8851317`, or the later `hakios-os` name),
because `d82c64a3` is not in the new history. Re-point the submodule only when we want new code
from it, and then port the two lost ir3 patches and the `hakios-drm` rename in one step.
