## Mesa 25.0.7 vs 26.2.6: keep 25.0.7

- OSMesa is removed (25.1.0, option gone in 25.2.0): our GL path (libOSMesa, topaz gl_osmesa.c, osmesa-es-profile) needs it.
- On 26.2.6 the topaz/HakiOS freedreno-darwin patch fails 4 hunks, 0001-turnip-darwin 10 hunks, 0002/0101 one each [V].
- 26.x hides 13 extensions on parts without multiview (maintenance4-9, spirv_1_4, subgroup_size_control, ...), which
  metal2vulkan uses [V relnotes/source].
- UBWC for the A610: on in both, same fields; no A610 UBWC fix in the range [V].
- No upstream fix matches a symptom we have seen. Cherry-pick single fixes onto 25.0.7 when one does (candidates: D32S8
  EARLY_Z_LATE_Z hang 26.2.0, GRAS_MODE_CNTL / shared-consts invalidate 26.2.6, a630_sqe CP_DRAW_AUTO 26.2.5).
- Found on the way [V `tu_device.cc:1073`]: Turnip 25.0.7 reports **Vulkan 1.0** for the A610 (`has_hw_multiview = False`),
  and metal2vulkan refuses devices below 1.2 (`device/create.rs:778`): Metal had no device on the A610. Fixed by
  `userland/laurel/metal/turnip-patches/0102-turnip-darwin-a6xx-gen1-vulkan13.patch`.
